# Data use disclaimer

The lit2bench corpus is provided **only for scientific research within the hackathon
"From Literature to Benchmarks"** (AI for Meta Science, Institut Pascal, Université Paris-Saclay,
23–27 November 2026).

## Access

- The corpus download link is **password-protected**. The password is given only to registered
  participants and organizers, who form the defined group of people doing this joint research.
- Do not share the link, the password or any part of the corpus with anyone outside that group.
- Do not redistribute the corpus or derived copies of the full texts, in whole or in part,
  for example in public repositories, notebooks, datasets or model releases.

## What the corpus contains

- **Metadata** of 1,894,408 INSPIRE-HEP records, as published by INSPIRE (CC0).
- **Full texts** extracted from PDFs on INSPIRE and arXiv. Copyright stays with the authors and
  publishers; only about a fifth of the papers carry an open license. They are made available here
  under the text and data mining exception for scientific research (EU Directive 2019/790, Art. 3;
  in Germany §60d UrhG; in France Art. L. 122-5-3 CPI), for non-commercial research only.
- **Personal data**: author names, affiliations, identifiers and some email addresses, as published
  with the papers. Use them only for the research tasks of the hackathon. Do not contact authors,
  build profiles of people, or publish these fields.

## After the hackathon

Delete your local copy of the corpus when the hackathon work is finished, unless the organizers
agree otherwise. Catalogues, code and results you produce (task → paper links, short quoted
evidence) are not affected.

This note describes how the organizers intend the corpus to be used; it is not legal advice.
